# 🎓 Prank Rickroll Student Login Website

A funny fake student registration & login page that ends in the ultimate betrayal — a Rickroll! 🤣

## 💡 What It Does

This project is a **fake student registration and login** webpage with a twist:

- The **Register** and **Login** buttons **run away** from your mouse unless you fill out the form properly! 🏃‍♂️💨
- When you *finally* manage to log in... **Boom! Rickrolled.** 🎵 Never gonna give you up...

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download the project.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Try to click "Register" or "Login" without filling the form — good luck catching the buttons! 😆
4. Once the form is filled, the buttons stay still.
5. Click "Login" and enjoy the surprise!

## 📁 Files

- `index.html` – Entry point with links to register and login pages.
- `login.html` – The fake login page that leads to the Rickroll.
- `register.js` – Makes the register button run away unless the form is filled.
- `login.js` – Handles the login form behavior and Rickroll redirect.
- `style.css` – Styles for layout and animations.
